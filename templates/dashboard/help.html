Help

How do I create a post?

Click the "Write" button in the navigation bar to open the editor. Enter a title, write your content in Markdown, and click Publish.

How do I edit a post?

Navigate to the post and click the action menu (three dots) next to the title, then select "Update".

How do I manage drafts?

Go to your Dashboard and click "Drafts" in the sidebar. You can edit, publish, or delete drafts from there.

How do I follow someone?

Visit a user's profile and click the "Follow" button. You can manage your connections in the Dashboard.
